---
id: data-export
title: Exporting your data
---
You can export all notes at any time, on every plan, from Settings > Data > Export. Exports are a ZIP file containing Markdown files and attachments.

Exports of more than 5 GB are prepared in the background and a download link is emailed to you. The link expires after 7 days.

Notebooks with end-to-end encryption are exported encrypted unless you unlock them first on the device that creates the export.
