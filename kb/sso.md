id: sso
title: Single sign-on
---
Single sign-on (SSO) with SAML 2.0 is available on the Business plan only. Supported identity providers include Okta, Microsoft Entra ID and Google Workspace.

To set up SSO, an administrator goes to Admin > Security > Single sign-on, uploads the identity provider's metadata file and verifies the company's email domain.

When SSO is enforced, members sign in through the identity provider and cannot use a password. SCIM provisioning is available on Business for creating and removing members automatically.
