---
id: upgrades-and-downgrades
title: Changing plans
---
Upgrades take effect immediately. When you upgrade in the middle of a billing period, you are charged a prorated amount for the rest of the period.

Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date. If your storage use is above the new plan's limit when the downgrade takes effect, you can still read and export all notes, but you cannot add new attachments until you are under the limit.

Moving from Business to Plus removes single sign-on. Members who signed in only through SSO must set a password before the downgrade.
