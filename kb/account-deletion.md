id: account-deletion
title: Deleting your account
---
You can delete your account from Settings > Account > Delete account. Deletion is permanent after a 14-day grace period, during which you can sign in again to cancel it.

After the grace period, all notes and attachments are removed from active systems within 30 days, and from backups within 90 days.

If you are the only administrator of a Business workspace, you must transfer administration or delete the workspace before you can delete your account.
