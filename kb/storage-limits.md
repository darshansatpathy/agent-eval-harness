title: Storage and attachment limits
---
Each attachment can be at most 200 MB on Plus and Business, and 25 MB on Free.

Storage counts attachments and note history. Deleted notes stay in the trash for 30 days and continue to count toward storage until the trash is emptied or the 30 days pass.

Extra storage can be bought on Plus and Business in blocks of 100 GB for $2 per month per block.
