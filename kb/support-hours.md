title: Getting support
---
Support is available by email on every plan. Free accounts get a first reply within 3 business days, Plus within 1 business day.

Business accounts also get live chat from 8:00 to 20:00 Central European Time on weekdays, with a first reply within 4 hours.

Support staff never ask for your password and cannot see the contents of end-to-end encrypted notebooks.
