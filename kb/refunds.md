id: refunds
title: Refunds
---
Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, annual plans are not refunded, but the plan stays active until the end of the paid year.

Monthly plans are not refunded. Cancelling a monthly plan stops the next renewal, and the plan stays active until the end of the current month.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Refunds cannot be issued as store credit.
