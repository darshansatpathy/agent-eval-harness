title: Security and encryption
---
All notes are encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or later and at rest with AES-256.

Business plans can turn on end-to-end encryption for selected notebooks. Notes in an end-to-end encrypted notebook cannot be searched on the web app and cannot be recovered by Nimbus support if the encryption key is lost.

Two-factor authentication is available on every plan and can be required for all members on Business.
