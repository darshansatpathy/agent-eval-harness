title: Billing cycle and invoices
---
Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period. Monthly plans are charged on the same day each month as the original purchase; annual plans on the anniversary of the purchase.

Invoices are emailed to the billing contact and are available under Settings > Billing > Invoices. Each invoice has an identifier of the form INV- followed by six digits, for example INV-204118.

If a payment fails, Nimbus Notes retries the card after 3 days and again after 7 days. The account moves to read-only mode 14 days after the first failed payment. No data is deleted while an account is read-only.
