title: Plans and pricing
---
Nimbus Notes has three plans: Free, Plus and Business.

The Free plan costs nothing and includes up to 3 notebooks, 1 GB of storage and sync on 2 devices.

The Plus plan costs $6 per user per month when billed annually, or $8 per user per month when billed monthly. Plus includes unlimited notebooks, 50 GB of storage per user and sync on unlimited devices.

The Business plan costs $14 per user per month, billed annually only. Business adds single sign-on (SAML), audit logs, a 99.9% uptime commitment and 200 GB of storage per user.

Prices exclude sales tax and VAT, which are added at checkout based on the billing address.
