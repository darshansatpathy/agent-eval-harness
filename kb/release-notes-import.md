title: Importing from other apps
---
Nimbus Notes can import from Evernote (ENEX files), OneNote and Markdown folders. Imports are available on every plan, including Free.

Imported notes keep their creation dates and tags. Attachments larger than the plan's attachment limit are skipped and listed in an import report.
