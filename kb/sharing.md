id: sharing
title: Sharing notes
---
Any note can be shared with a link. Link sharing can be view-only or allow comments; editing through a link is not possible.

Notebooks can be shared with specific people as viewer, commenter or editor. On Free, a notebook can be shared with at most 5 people; on Plus and Business there is no limit.

Business administrators can turn off public link sharing for the whole workspace under Admin > Security.
